How the score works

The score blends muscle activity, median-frequency drop from your fresh baseline, discomfort, load duration and the muscle’s typical fatigue profile. Poor sleep and low hydration add a penalty. Fast-twitch muscles receive longer-rest and glycolytic recovery guidance; slow-twitch postural muscles receive micro-break and circulation guidance.

The sample dashboard value of 35.93% activity and 53 Hz is labelled “Normal”. A brief spike near 97% and a drop to 0 Hz may indicate electrode movement or contact loss, so re-seat the electrodes before trusting those points.

Educational tool for kinesiology and ergonomics, not a medical diagnosis. Persistent pain, numbness or swelling needs a clinician.
